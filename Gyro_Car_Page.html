UPPER STORY

Gyro-Car Design

Upper Story | June - September 2025

Context

This project was developed as part of a product design internship at Upper Story, where I worked on reimagining the next generation of gyroscopic RC cars for educational and recreational use.

Goal

Engineer a Gyro-RC car with a flywheel delivering 35% greater output than the previous model while maximizing battery efficiency.

Front View
Back View

Steps Taken

Design

  • Redesign/CAD the entire car in Inventor
  • Raise the flywheel, completely changing the geometry
  • Increase battery size
  • Change to snap-in "easy swap" battery system
  • Design custom SMT battery clips with sheet metal
  • Design a battery charger from the ground-up

Manufacturing

  • Rapid prototype using resin prints and CNC
  • Manufacture and post process battery chargers using SLA and airbrushes
  • Reprogram the car with new IMU Library
  • Incorporate auto-shutoff and battery charge states display
  • DFM: every part is 2-piece injection moldable

Successfully increased flywheel output power by 49% while implementing a 2-piece injection moldable design for all components. The new snap-in battery system reduced swap time by an estimated 10 minutes, significantly improving user experience.
